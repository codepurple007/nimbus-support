# Reset your Nimbus password

To reset the password on a Nimbus account:

1. Open the Nimbus sign-in page.
2. Select **Forgot password**.
3. Enter the email address on the account.
4. Open the email from Nimbus (it expires after 30 minutes).
5. Choose a new password that is at least 10 characters.

You will stay signed in on the device where you complete the reset. Other devices stay signed in until you sign them out from **Settings → Security → Sessions**. See the Sessions article if you want to sign out everywhere.

Nimbus support cannot see or set your password. If you do not receive the email, check spam, then open a ticket.
