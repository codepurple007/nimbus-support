# Sessions and devices

Each signed-in browser or phone is a session.

- Resetting your password does **not** automatically sign out other devices.
- To sign out everywhere: **Settings → Security → Sessions → Sign out all other sessions**.
- You can revoke a single device from that same list.
- Two-factor authentication (2FA), if enabled, is still required after a password reset.

If you think someone else used your account, reset your password, sign out all sessions, and open a ticket.
