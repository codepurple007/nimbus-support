# Shipping and delivery

Nimbus hardware kits ship from the Nairobi warehouse.

- Standard shipping inside Kenya: **3–5 business days**.
- East Africa regional: **7–12 business days**.
- You receive a tracking number by email when the kit leaves the warehouse.
- “Late” means the estimated window has passed. We need the **order number** (for example `#1042`) before we can check status.
- If a kit is more than 3 business days past the window, we reship or refund the shipping fee. That decision is made by a human after the order is found.

The chat agent must not invent a tracking status. If there is no order number, ask for it or open a ticket.
