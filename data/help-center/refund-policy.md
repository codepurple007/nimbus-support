# Refund policy

Nimbus subscriptions can be refunded within **14 days** of the original purchase, no questions asked.

- **Inside 14 days:** you are eligible for a full refund of the last charge. Ask in chat or email billing@nimbus.example. A human from billing completes the refund. The support agent cannot send money.
- **After 14 days:** you are not eligible for a refund. You may still cancel so you are not charged again. Unused time in the current period is not returned.
- Hardware kits (Nimbus Hub and accessories) follow the same 14-day window, unopened or with all parts returned.
- Chargebacks should not be used instead of this policy. Open a ticket if a charge looks wrong.

This policy is the source of truth. Support must cite it and must not invent exceptions.
