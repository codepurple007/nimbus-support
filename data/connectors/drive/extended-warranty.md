# Extended warranty

Nimbus Hub and Security Key include the warranty months listed in the product
catalog. Customers can buy a **24-month extended warranty** for the Hub only.

- Extended warranty SKU: NIM-HUB-EW
- Price: 29 USD
- Must be purchased within 30 days of the original Hub order
- Does not cover cables, mounts, or accidental damage
- File a ticket for a warranty claim. The agent cannot approve replacements.
